About

The worlds we inherited are still here. We study their architecture, their memory and the futures they might hold.

What we investigate

We look at the ordinary built environments shaped by growth, consumer culture and ideas of progress. Shopping centres, new towns, infrastructures and the spaces in between. Some are thriving. Others are changing or disappearing.

We ask how these places were made, what they meant to the people who used them, and what can be learned from the ways they persist and transform.

How we work

ARTIFICE is an independent research and creative practice founded by architects Doriann Delaive and Yasmine Kaci Chaouche.

We work across architectural surveys, archives, interviews, drawing, scanning, photography, film, sound and design. Research can become a publication, an image, a spatial experiment or an architectural project.

Our respective research interests in architectural production, obsolescence, ordinary heritage and social memory come together through shared investigations.
